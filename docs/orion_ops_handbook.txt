Orion Retail Data Platform - Operations Handbook v3.2 (fictional, capstone edition)

S1 Nightly load. The nightly load runs between 01:00 and 05:00 UTC. Any job still running at 05:00 UTC is a late load and must be reported to the platform on-call.
S2 Retention. Raw data is kept for 30 days. Curated (silver and gold) data is kept for 7 years. Audit logs are kept for 13 months.
S3 Severity. Sev 1: dashboard or load down for all users. Sev 2: wrong figures on a P0 report. Sev 3: a single feed delayed. Sev 4: cosmetic.
S4 Production access. Production access needs approval from the data owner and the security team and lasts 90 days. Contractors never get access to Restricted data.
S5 Quality gates. A load fails the gate if row counts differ from source by more than 0.5%. Rows with null or duplicate primary keys are moved to quarantine and never loaded to silver.
S6 Reruns and backfills. A backfill of more than 7 days needs a change ticket and can only start after 20:00 UTC.
S7 Test environments. Test environments use masked data only. Production data is never copied to test.
S8 Releases. Releases go out on Tuesdays and Thursdays after a green regression run.
S9 Alerting. Failed jobs alert the on-call engineer by page; late loads alert by email.
S10 Support. Sev 1 and Sev 2 incidents are handled 24x7. Sev 3 and Sev 4 are handled next business day.
S11 Classification. Customer names, emails and birth dates are Restricted. Order amounts are Internal.
S12 Sales mart. The sales mart holds 4.2 TB. Any query scanning more than 1 TB needs approval from the data owner.
